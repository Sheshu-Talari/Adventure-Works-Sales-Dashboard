# Adventure-Works-Sales-Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing the Adventure Works Cycles dataset to understand business performance, customer behavior, and sales trends across different regions.

Adventure Works is a multinational bicycle manufacturing company aiming to expand its market share, optimize sales strategies, and reduce operational costs.

---

## 📊 Tools Used
- Excel
- Power BI
- Tableau
- SQL

---

## 📈 Objectives
- Analyze sales performance across regions
- Identify top-performing products and customers
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Evaluate business growth opportunities
- Support decision-making with data-driven insights

---

## 📁 Project Structure
- Excel Analysis  
- Power BI Dashboard  
- Tableau Dashboard
- SQL Queries 
- Dataset  
- Dashboard Screenshots  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Identified top revenue-generating regions and products  
- High-value customers contribute significantly to total sales  
- Certain product categories dominate overall revenue  
- Regional trends highlight opportunities for market expansion  

---

## 🎯 Business Impact
This analysis helps Adventure Works:
- Improve targeting of high-value customers  
- Optimize product strategy  
- Enhance regional sales performance  
- Support strategic decision-making  

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
The project provides actionable insights into sales performance and customer behavior, helping the company make informed business decisions and improve profitability.
